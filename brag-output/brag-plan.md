# Zeus Protect — brag plan

**What it is:** Zeus's own Health & Safety Hub. One portal for training, incidents, risk assessments, fire safety, COSHH, equipment, permits and inspections, plus a phone app for staff on the floor.
**Who it's for:** Warehouse and office staff, who report hazards and complete training on their phones, and the H&S manager, who sees everything on one dashboard.
**What sets it apart:** A hazard spotted on the floor takes one sentence and three quick screens to report, and it goes straight into the portal for review.
**Hook:** "Someone's climbing the racking." This comes from the app's own seed incidents.
**Visual hook:** The light-theme phone flow cuts to the dark navy admin dashboard, and its stat cards pop in one by one.
**Tone:** `default`: punchy and clean, with soft transitions at 120 BPM so cuts land on the beat.
**Share caption:** Spot it, report it, sorted.

## Visual identity
- Navy `#060d2e` / `#0d1f5c` / `#152370`, accent `#2563eb`/`#3b82f6`, amber `#f59e0b`, red `#ef4444`, green `#10b981`
- Barlow typeface (the app's font), Z app icon from `public/icons`
- All UI is real: screenshots of the running app (seed data, legacy sign-in, Supabase stubbed). Real staff names swapped for fictional ones.

## Sound
A minor, 120 BPM: a soft pad and a pluck arpeggio (Am–F–C–G), with kick and bass coming in at the phone scene. Taps are soft pitched plucks in key, typing ticks sit low in the mix, whooshes are filtered noise on the cuts, and a bell chime in key plays on "Sent" and on the logo.
